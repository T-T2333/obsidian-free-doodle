## Improvements

- **Handwriting now auto-replaces** — finish a character and pause briefly (~0.9s); recognition runs automatically and the top candidate is swapped in as neat glyph text. No more clicking ✨ then picking a candidate for the happy path. The sparkle button remains as a manual fallback (shows the candidate picker when you want a different character).
- Auto runs are silent on empty/failed recognition (your ink stays put); the Diagnostics log still records every attempt (`auto=true` / `auto=false`).
- Leaving handwriting mode, clearing ink, closing the view, or unloading the plugin cancels any pending auto-recognize timer.
